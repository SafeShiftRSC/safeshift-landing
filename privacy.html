Privacy

What happens to your information when you contact us.

Short version: the enquiry form sends your details straight to Rob. Nothing is sold, nothing is added to a marketing list, and pageview counts are anonymous. The rest of this page is the long version.

What the enquiry form collects

01

Name, company, and the email or phone number you give us.

02

Whatever you write in the "what's broken right now" field.

03

Submissions are delivered by Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, straight to enquiries@safeshift.com.au. It's a dedicated mailbox, but only Rob reads it. No sales team.

What happens to it

01

It's used to reply to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to scope and run the install. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside SafeShift Systems.

02

It's kept for as long as it's useful to that (an active conversation, an active install, or reasonable follow-up after), then deleted.

03

Email us any time to see what we hold on you, correct it, or have it deleted.

Site analytics

01

This site uses GoatCounter for pageview counts. It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address or full browser identifier, and cannot identify individual visitors. It tells us how many people looked at the page, not who.
