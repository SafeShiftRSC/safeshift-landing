How it works

From yes to running, then running on its own.

Here is exactly what happens after you say yes, and exactly what the system does once it is live.

Getting installed. Four stages.

Two to four weeks from the preflight call to go-live, depending on how quickly your IT access comes through.
01
Preflight call
Fifteen minutes to confirm admin access, your nominated System Owner, site count, and scope. No confirmation, no install proceeds.
02
Build
The form, the tracker, and the automation are built directly inside your own Microsoft 365 tenant. Five to seven business days.
03
Test and sign-off
An end-to-end test with your System Owner. Every item on the Definition of Done below is confirmed before anything is handed over.
04
Handover
Your System Owner gets the handover guide and a 30-day hypercare window. After that, it is yours, with no ongoing dependency on us to keep it running.

Once it is live. Six steps, automatic after the first.

A worker submits a form. Everything after that happens without anyone remembering to do it.
01
Report
A worker submits a short Microsoft Form. No new app, no new login, no separate account to set up.
02
File
The submission creates a tracker record automatically inside Microsoft Lists. Nothing gets re-typed by hand.
03
Assign
An owner is assigned and notified the moment it is logged. No one has to remember to tell them.
04
Enforce
Due dates are set and tracked. Overdue items are flagged automatically, not caught in a monthly review.
05
Evidence
A PDF record generates on every submission and files itself into SharePoint, ready if you are ever asked to show it.
06
Report up
A weekly digest lands in the System Owner's inbox. What is open, what is overdue, what is new. No one has to go looking for it.
