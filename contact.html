Before you write in

A few things people usually ask.

Does this replace SharePoint or Microsoft 365?

No. It is built inside what you already have. No new software to license, no new platform for anyone to learn.

What do you need from us before you can start?

Confirmed admin access, a nominated System Owner, and agreement on scope. No owner, no install. It is a hard gate, not a formality.

How long does an install actually take?

Two to four weeks from the preflight call to go-live, depending on how quickly your IT access comes through. Two rounds of tweaks are included, everything past that is a separate product build.

What happens after the first 30 days?

Thirty days of hypercare is included with every install. After that, support is paid or covered under a care plan. There is no open-ended free support.

Does this guarantee we are compliant?

No, and I will not tell you otherwise. We install systems. You keep WHS responsibility. Nobody can guarantee compliance, us included.
